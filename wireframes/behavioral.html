Leadership Difficulty: High

"Tell me about a time you led a team through significant uncertainty or ambiguity — where there was no clear path forward."

McKinsey is testing: Did you drive the outcome, or just participate? Was your leadership clear? What was the actual impact?

STAR Framework

S — SITUATION

Set context in 1-2 sentences. Name the company, role, stakes. Make it tangible.

e.g. "At Deloitte, 3 weeks before client delivery, our project lead resigned unexpectedly."

T — TASK

Your specific role and responsibility. Use "I" not "we." What was yours to own?

e.g. "I was the only analyst with visibility across all three workstreams."

A — ACTION

Specific steps you took. This is where leadership shows. Be concrete — what exactly did you do?

e.g. "I immediately called a 30-min sync with all 4 team members, reprioritized the workplan, and took ownership of the client slide deck."

R — RESULT

Quantify the outcome. Business impact + personal growth signal.

e.g. "Delivered on time. Client extended engagement by 6 months. I was offered a return full-time offer."

McKinsey PEI: What they're looking for

✓

You drove the outcome — not the team, not the manager

✓

High-stakes situation with real ambiguity

✓

Specific actions, not vague "I coordinated" language

✗

Avoid: "We as a team..." — they want YOUR individual contribution

✗

Avoid: Generic stories (group projects, club presidency without impact)
