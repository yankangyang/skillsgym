Break-even ★★★

"A product costs $240 to produce and sells for $300. Fixed costs are $18,000 per month. How many units must be sold to break even?"

≈
units
💡
Hint

Break-even = Fixed Costs ÷ Contribution Margin per unit.
Contribution Margin = Selling Price − Variable Cost per unit.

300 units

How to get there:
Contribution margin = $300 − $240 = $60
Break-even = $18,000 ÷ $60 = 300 units

💡

Interviewer tip: Always anchor contribution margin first. In case interviews, break-even questions test whether you can identify variable vs. fixed costs — not just the arithmetic.
